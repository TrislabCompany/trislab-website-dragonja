---
title: Accessibility statement
description: How accessible this site is and how to report a problem.
---

The dragonja.si website aims to be usable by everyone. The target is conformance with WCAG 2.1, level AA.

## How it is checked

Every change to the site is checked automatically before it is published, with the axe and pa11y tools, on a computer and on a 320-pixel-wide phone, where no page may scroll sideways. Every photo has a description in every language, and the site works with a keyboard and without JavaScript.

## Known limitations

- The map on Visit us is an image. The same attractions are listed under it as links.
- The Croatian, Italian and German texts are translations still being reviewed by a native speaker.

## Feedback

If something on the site is hard to use, write to [hello@dragonja.si](mailto:hello@dragonja.si). We reply within a few working days.
