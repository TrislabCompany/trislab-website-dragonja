## What the site collects

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or trackers and loads no content from other servers. It has no forms and stores nothing in your browser.

## Hosting

The pages are served by Cloudflare as a processor. To deliver and protect the site it processes the technical data of each request (IP address, time, page requested and browser). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running the site securely. Visits are counted from Cloudflare's traffic statistics, which do not identify individual visitors.

## E-mail

If you write to us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we do not pass them on.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to object. Write to the address above. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.
