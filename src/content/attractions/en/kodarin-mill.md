The Kodarin mill near Koštabona was built around 1800. The Kodarin family saved it from collapse; today it is a farm with rooms to stay and a venue for weddings.

About 40 mills once worked on the Dragonja. Two have been restored: the Kodarin mill and the Mazurin mill at Trsek.
