The Supot, a right tributary of the Dragonja, falls about 10 metres over a ledge of flysch. Locally the fall is also called Pr' Raka. A large stand of maidenhair fern grows below it, and it is proposed as a natural monument.

The high fall runs only after rain and dries out in the dry season. Lower down, where the gravel road from Koštabona to Dragonja crosses the stream, a smaller fall of about 5 metres with a pool flows all year.
