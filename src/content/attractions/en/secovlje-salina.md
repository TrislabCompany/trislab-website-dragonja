The saltworks lie along the mouth of the Dragonja and cover about 650 hectares. A visit to the park needs an entrance ticket.

The park has two entrances. A paved path leads to Lera from the main road between Lucija and the Sečovlje border crossing. The Salt Pans Museum at Fontanigge is reached by the dirt road along the Dragonja. A guided tour of Lera lasts at least an hour and a half; groups book it ahead.
