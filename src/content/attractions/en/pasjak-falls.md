The Pasjak, a left tributary of the Dragonja below Topolovec, runs through a gorge with waterfalls and tufa. The gorge is registered as a natural value.

The falls lie below St Jerome's church, right on the Croatian border. The path starts at Belvedur near Topolovec. There is most water in spring and autumn.
