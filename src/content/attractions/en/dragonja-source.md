The Dragonja starts as a fan of small springs in steep gullies between the villages of Zabavlje, Popetre, Poletiči, Butari, Trebeše and Gradin. Within 4 kilometres the streams drop some 150 metres and join south-east of Trsek.

From there the river runs 30 kilometres to the sea, and its lower course is the border with Croatia.
