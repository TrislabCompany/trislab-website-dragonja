Truške lies on a hill above the upper Dragonja valley, south of Marezige, in the municipality of Koper. It is first recorded in 1067.

The first church, of the Virgin Mary, stood in the hamlet of Vršič; a new church, easier to reach, was built in the 16th century. The village is made up of the hamlets Kortine, Vršič, Gonjači, Mačji hrib, Pr kapelce and Bočaji.
