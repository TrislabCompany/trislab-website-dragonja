The Cingarela falls in several steps into a pool and then runs through a narrow gorge. It is formed by the Poganja stream, also called Argilla, a tributary of the Dragonja that flows mostly through Croatia.

It lies on the Croatian side of the valley, between Momjan and Kaštel.
