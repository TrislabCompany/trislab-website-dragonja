Boršt stands on top of the Varda hill, about 353 m above sea level.

The church of St Roch and St Sebastian dates from the 13th century. It was once a branch church of St Cantianus in Truške and now belongs to the parish of Marezige. Tradition places the Roman settlement of Elpidium nearby.
