The walk starts in Sečovlje, follows the edge of the Dragonja valley and climbs towards Nova vas nad Dragonjo. On the way it opens views over the salt pans.

At the end the bell tower of Nova vas watches over the valley, among stone houses, narrow lanes and olive trees.
