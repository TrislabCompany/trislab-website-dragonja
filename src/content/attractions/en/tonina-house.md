Tona's house is a 19th-century stone house with an olive mill and press on the ground floor and the living rooms upstairs. It is named after its last owner, Antonia Gorela, who lived there until 1961.

Today it is an ethnological collection of the Sergej Mašera Maritime Museum in Piran. It is closed in winter, from 1 December to 31 March.
