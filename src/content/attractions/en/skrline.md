Among the most scenic waterfalls of the Dragonja are those at Škrline, where the Rokava, its largest right-hand tributary, flows into it.

An old stone bridge crosses the river between Škrline and Brič. The Dragonja often dries up in summer, so the falls are at their best from autumn to spring.
