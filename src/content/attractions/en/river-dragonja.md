The Dragonja is the third-longest river in Istria and the largest Slovenian river that flows into the Adriatic. It is about 30 km long and flows entirely over flysch, without passing through any settlement.

In summer it often dries up. Its lowest part is a natural monument. The river is first recorded as *Argao*.
