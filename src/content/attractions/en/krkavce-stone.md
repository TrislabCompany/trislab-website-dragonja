The Krkavče stone, also called the menhir of Krkavče, is an unworked stone about 2.5 metres tall, of which about 1.5 metres stands above ground. A nude figure with outstretched arms and a halo like the sun is carved on it; its top is broken off.

Its age is disputed. The original is kept in the Koper Regional Museum, and a copy stands on the site.
