# dragonja.si: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied to the product repository as `docs/brief.md` by `handoff-apply`. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

A presentation site for the village of Dragonja and the Dragonja valley in Slovenian Istria, rebuilt on `tl-template-website` in the repository `trislab-website-dragonja`. It replaces today's Angular single-page app. Trislab owns and publishes it. Its readers are tourists, local people and local organisations; it helps them find what to see and do in the valley and how to get there. Success is measured by the number of visitors. There is no sign-in, no stored data and no form: contact is an e-mail link.

The site is also the pilot of the template's init script: its first pull request is made by `pnpm init-site` from a generated `site.yaml`.

**A custom design.** The brief is "impress us": a new design for a nature and culture destination, mobile first, led by large photographs of the valley. Today's colours (dark blue #2c3e50, blues #3498db and #2980b9, light background #f8f9fa) and the Poppins font are the starting point, not a constraint. The tone is friendly. The init pull request sets a darker accent (#21618c) because white on #2980b9 fails the contrast check.

## Design direction

The template is only the frame (build, checks, languages, legal pages, hosting). The look is the site's own, in its `src/` (components, layouts, pages, styles).

- **Feel:** a calm nature and culture destination, led by large photos of the valley, with generous white space. From the reference sites:
  - soca-valley.com: the way from inspiration to action, with large image cards;
  - lesachtal.com: one clear identity and seasonal advice;
  - kpss.si: practical visit information.
- **Colours** come from the logo: river blue, hill green and earth brown, on the warm white of Istrian stone. The bright logo colours are for shapes only. Text and buttons use the deep tones (deep river blue #0b5878 for buttons and links, ink #1c2a2e for text), and every pair passes 4.5:1. The "How to get here" band and the footer use a dark river tone (#102a33).
- **Type:** Fraunces, a soft old-style serif, for headings, and Inter for text. Both are self-hosted.
- **Photos:**
  - shown full-bleed in the hero and as tall cards with the text on a dark gradient;
  - AVIF at a few widths, lazy below the first screen;
  - every photo is registered with its alternative text in every language and its credit, and each page lists the credits of the photos it shows;
  - photos of the real places come from today's site or from freely licensed Wikimedia Commons photos.
- **Home page, top to bottom:**
  1. the hero: a full-screen photo, the title "Dolina Dragonje", one sentence, and two buttons ("Discover the valley", "Plan your visit"); the header lies over it;
  2. three ways in (Nature, Culture, Activities) as large photo cards that lead to the attractions grouped by kind;
  3. the story of the valley: four numbered steps (the river, the mills, the hamlets, the border), each with its source;
  4. six featured attractions as photo cards;
  5. when to come: autumn to spring, when the river flows, and summer, when it often dries up;
  6. how to get here: by car, by bike on the Parenzana, on foot, in a dark band;
  7. the photo credits and the footer.
- **Other pages:**
  - the attractions page groups the attractions by kind, with jump links to each kind;
  - each attraction page opens with its photo, then where it is, the text and its sources;
  - About opens with a smaller photo head, then one chapter per theme (the river, the mills, the hamlets, the border), each a photo beside its text and sources, alternating sides;
  - Visit us opens the same way, then how to get here (a dark band), good to know (parking, the border, the salt pans, summer), when to come, the map with numbered attractions and a list of them, the gallery and the contact.
- **Navigation:** Attractions, About, Visit us. On a phone the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript. Contact is in the footer.
- **Design rules,** checked by `pnpm test` where a machine can:
  - a photo shows at most once on a page, and every photo on a page has its credit there;
  - every fact cites its source; when two sources disagree, the detail is left out;
  - no placeholder text reaches any page, in any language, the legal pages included.

## Pages or modules

Pages are built from the template's sections (`page-sections`):

- **Home:** the valley in one large image and one sentence, the main attractions, a short story section (the river, the mills, the villages, the border), and how to get here.
- **About:** the village and the valley: the river (about 30 km, it dries up in parts in summer), the mills, the hamlets, the history, the Slovenian–Croatian border.
- **Attractions:** a list with a page per attraction (`content-collections`, list `attractions`): nature, culture, activities and places around the valley. About 10 to 20 items, changed rarely. Each item has a name, a kind (nature, culture, activity, place nearby), a photo, a short text and its location.
- **Visit us:** how to get here (car, bus, bicycle on the Parenzana trail), where to park, the best seasons, a map (`location-map`) and a photo gallery (`photo-gallery`).
- **Privacy notice and accessibility statement** (`legal-pages`). No imprint.

The attraction candidates, with their sources, are the starting list: the river and its valley, the Škrline waterfalls and the stone bridge, the Rokava stream, the Stena rock outcrop, the Sečovlje Salina Nature Park and its Salt Museum, the Mazurin and Kodarin mills, St Roch's church in Boršt, the Roman finds, the Parenzana trail, the valley-edge walk, Truške and Labor, Nova vas nad Dragonjo, Padna, Sveti Peter. Trislab chooses the final list.

## Languages

Slovenian (default), English, Croatian, Italian and German. Each language has its own URL with `hreflang` and `x-default` (`multi-language`). Today's site has texts in sl, en, hr and it; German is new. The texts are written for tourists in Slovenian and English first, then machine-translated, and reviewed before production. Every fact (lengths, dates, numbers) cites its source; a fact that can't be checked against a source is left out.

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** dragonja.si. www.dragonja.si redirects to it permanently at the Cloudflare edge.
- **Environments** on Cloudflare Pages: `dev` at dev.dragonja.si (every merge to `main`), `staging` at staging.dragonja.si (release tags), both behind Cloudflare Access; `production` at dragonja.si, promoted from `staging` after the release approver's sign-off. The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Edge:** dragonja.si is a zone in Trislab's Cloudflare account (tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/dragonja.si/`). At the cutover the apex record to the old host goes; the mail records (MX, mail, webmail, autodiscover, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and the Google site verification stay.
- **Search Console** (`search-console`): the domain property `sc-domain:dragonja.si`, owned by Trislab's role account, verified by the record `google` in tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/dragonja.si/dragonja.si.yaml` and linked in its `catalog/domains.yaml`. Keep the record through the cutover. The sitemap is `https://dragonja.si/sitemap-index.xml`: Trislab submits it after the cutover and checks the site in Search Console 4–6 weeks later.

## Integrations and outside services

None. The map is a static image built from OpenStreetMap data, the font is self-hosted, and there are no statistics: the Content-Security-Policy stays at the template's default (`'self'` only), and no visitor data reaches a third party. Visitor numbers come from Cloudflare's own traffic statistics.

## Quality baseline

The template's whole baseline, no exceptions: `responsive-layout` (mobile first, no sideways scrolling at 320px), `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget`, `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. Uptime alerts come when the platform has them. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Assets

- **Available on today's site:** the logo (`assets/images/logo.jpg`, a JPG only: draw an SVG from it), the photos in `assets/images/` (`hero.webp` is the share image), the texts in four languages (`assets/i18n/<lang>.json`).
- **To make:** the SVG logo and favicon; the texts for the attractions and the four pages; the German translation; the static map image; the privacy notice from the template's draft.
- **Placeholders:** a missing photo gets a placeholder that is replaced before production.
- **Photos of the earlier site:** two of its five photos show no known place (a family on bikes in a forest, a river that may not be the Dragonja) and are not used. The hero and the stone house are used. Where they were taken, and who may publish them, is checked before production.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page and every attraction exists in all five languages, with `hreflang`, a canonical address and a sitemap entry.
- `ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget, the browser check (no sideways scrolling at 320px, axe-core), the claims check, the design check (no photo twice on a page, every photo credited, no placeholder text) and the `security.txt` check.
- The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from another origin, the map included.
- `/about`, `/attractions` and `/visit-us` and www.dragonja.si redirect permanently to the new pages.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the structured data of a page and of an attraction.
- SEO (owner: the development team, unless named): the Lighthouse SEO score passes in `ci`; every page and attraction has one share image with alt text in all five languages; dev and staging stay behind sign-in and out of search. After the cutover (owner: Trislab): the sitemap is submitted in Search Console, and the old addresses show as redirected, not as errors.
- Before production: no placeholder image is left, every fact has been checked against its source, the translations are reviewed, the privacy notice is in place, and the release approver accepts staging.
- After the cutover: mail to the domain still arrives.

## Open questions

- Today's site shows a phone number and office hours that look like placeholders; the new site shows only the hello mailbox.
- The search terms (Dragonja, the valley and the river in five languages, Istrian villages, the Parenzana) have no measured search volume yet; check them with a keyword tool before launch.
- Some attraction sources must be found again before their text is written (the Kodarin mill, St Roch's church, Truške and Labor), and the Stena rock outcrop has only a weak source.
